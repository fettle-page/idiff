## How this was made

Installed silently with winget on a fresh GitHub Actions runner (win25-vs2026 20261007.264.1) by idiff 1.0.0 on 2026-10-15, then uninstalled silently. The app itself was never started. [Workflow run](https://github.com/fettle-page/idiff/actions/runs/38780384255). idiff only reads; see the README for how it decides what belongs to the app.

What counts: background services, scheduled tasks, drivers and inbound firewall rules are the receipt's own warning lines of those names. A startup entry is the "Starts with Windows" line; a service that starts with Windows is counted as a service only. A scheduled task counts as daily when its line says it runs every day. Root certificates are the "Adds a trusted root certificate" lines. Leftovers are the lines under "After uninstall" that say something is still there.
